Scenario 3: Two Streets, Two Lives

Two contrasting neighbourhoods. The same person, with the same health advice. How easy is it to follow? (about 70 minutes)

Learning outcomes

  1. Identify the wider determinants of health in a built environment
  2. Critique individual-level health advice in the context of structural constraints
  3. Apply the Dahlgren & Whitehead model to a real place
  4. Propose interventions at individual, community and policy level
Content note: Deprivation. Some students may live in, or come from, neighbourhoods like Environment B. Avoid language that blames people.

Questions (3), click to show

If we tell Sam to eat healthily, exercise more and attend their appointments, how easy is it to do that?

What stands in the way?

Is this about choice?

Reveal 1
The nearest large supermarket is two buses away. The corner shop sells little fresh produce.
Reveal 2
Sam's shift pattern changes weekly. The GP surgery only offers appointments between 9am and 5pm.
Reveal 3
The park nearby has broken lighting, and Sam doesn't feel safe there after dark.
Reveal 4
The flat is damp and the children share a room.
