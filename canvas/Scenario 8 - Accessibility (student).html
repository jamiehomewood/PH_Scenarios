Scenario 8: Accessibility Audit

Groups audit everyday environments from assigned perspectives, including wheelchair user, visual impairment, autism, frailty and others. (about 80 minutes)

Learning outcomes

  1. Conduct a structured accessibility audit of a public environment
  2. Apply the social model of disability
  3. Identify duties under the Equality Act 2010, including reasonable adjustments
  4. Prioritise realistic recommendations for improvement
Content note: Don't simulate impairments. Students audit from a perspective, using evidence and empathy.

What does the law require?

Reveal 1
Social model: people are disabled by barriers in society, not by their impairment.
Reveal 2
Equality Act 2010: service providers have an anticipatory duty to make reasonable adjustments.
Reveal 3
Accessible Information Standard: information must be provided in a format people can use.
