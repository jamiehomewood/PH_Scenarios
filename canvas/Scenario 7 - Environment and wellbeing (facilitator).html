Scenario 7: Mental Health: Seeing the Environment Differently

This scenario does not simulate mental illness. Students compare five environments and their effect on stress, isolation, wellbeing and help-seeking. (about 65 minutes)

Learning outcomes

  1. Identify environmental factors that affect stress and wellbeing
  2. Explain the relationship between social isolation and mental health
  3. Apply the Five Ways to Wellbeing framework
  4. Recognise barriers to help-seeking in different settings
Content note: Mental health. Give a content note at the start, make opt-out easy, and signpost student wellbeing services at the end.

Facilitator notes
  • Workload, control, support, noise, job insecurity. The HSE Management Standards cover six areas of work design linked to stress.
